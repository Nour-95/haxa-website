Escape the Forest

Immerse yourself in a horror experience like no other. Solve the puzzles, survive the forest, escape the legend.

Discover Now

Game Zones: he forest is divided into several “stations” or themed zones. Each zone represents a part of the story and contains puzzles or props. Trail Map or Guided Path: Participants follow a predefined path, sometimes with a map or guided by an actor/performer. Live Actors: Actors play roles such as witches, lost villagers, cursed spirits, or ritual leaders. They may interact, spook, or even help players. Clues & Props: Hidden inside tree hollows, under fake rocks, or hanging from branches in sealed scrolls. Found through UV light, sound cues, or coded messages burned into bark or etched into stone. Ritual sites with bones, candles, books, etc. that hint at the next move. Tools Provided: Lanterns, walkie-talkies, maps, or magical talismans. Time Limit: 60–90 minutes, tracked via actor or device. Safety and Control: Staff/actors follow from a distance and intervene only when needed. Emergency exits and communication tools are part of the safety system. Paths are cleared, but look wild.
